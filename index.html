Calendar Automation

Calendar Automation is a private, personal-use assistant that helps its owner manage their own Google Calendar and Gmail. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's calendar to build daily agendas and reminders.
  • Creates and updates events on the owner's calendar when asked.
  • Reads the owner's email to find items such as bills, confirmations, and reminders.
  • Sends notification emails (for example, a daily agenda) to the owner.

The app runs on the owner's own hardware. It is used only by its owner.
