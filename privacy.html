Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Calendar Automation is a personal-use application operated by and for a single individual (the "owner"). It is not offered to the public and no other people's accounts are connected to it.

Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google's sign-in screen, the app may access the owner's Google account data:

  • Gmail (read-only): to find messages such as bills, confirmations, and reminders.
  • Gmail (send): to send notification emails, such as a daily agenda, to the owner.
  • Google Calendar: to read the owner's events and to create or update events at the owner's request.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide the features above to the owner.
  • The app runs on the owner's own private server. Data and access tokens are stored there, and tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Selected text may be sent to an AI model provider only to carry out a request made by the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections. Stored data is deleted by the owner from their own server.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through this site's GitHub issue tracker.
